Three maps, chosen before the round

Ground & Sky (default), Rocky Hill and Blue Sea. The picker sits on the menu
above PLAY: three chips, the chosen one lit, with a line under it naming what
lives there. The choice is remembered in localStorage and passed to GameScene,
which builds its spawn pool from that map only. The map name shows in the HUD.

Species are assigned by type in mapForTypes(), checked sea -> hill -> default,
so a Water/Flying Pokémon is a sea creature and the default map catches
everything else. That keeps the Pokédex fillable: 38 ground, 13 sea, 7 rocky.

Rocky Hill only had Onix, so it also gets Sandshrew, Diglett, Geodude, Graveler,
Golem and Cubone (all Gen 1) with the usual two facts each. 58 species total,
and the dex grid handles the wider roster at 10 columns.

Backgrounds are still the crayon meadow on all three maps; drawMeadow() is the
one place to branch when the new art exists.
